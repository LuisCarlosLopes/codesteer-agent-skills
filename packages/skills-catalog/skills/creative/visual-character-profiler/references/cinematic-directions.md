# Direções Cinematográficas

Quatro direções de iluminação e mood, cada uma otimizada para um tipo de campanha. Cada direção traz um **Prompt Injection** (entra no bloco `[CINEMATOGRAPHY]` do template) e um **Negative Injection** (entra na linha `Negative:`). Não misture injections de direções diferentes no mesmo prompt.

---

## [A] Editorial Premium (High-End Campaign)

**Quando usar:** moda, luxo, beleza, lifestyle premium.

**Prompt Injection:**
```
shot on RED V-RAPTOR 8K with Cooke Anamorphic 75mm lens, f/2.0 aperture,
Rembrandt lighting with soft key light from upper left at 45 degrees,
warm fill light at 1:3 ratio, subtle rim light separating subject from background,
natural skin texture with visible pores and peach fuzz, subsurface scattering
on skin showing warm undertones, individual hair strands catching light,
shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh, muted color palette with lifted blacks,
fashion editorial color grading, subject fills 70% of frame
```

**Negative Injection:**
```
watermark, blurry, deformed, extra fingers, extra limbs, plastic skin, mannequin,
illustration, 3d render, cartoon, anime, doll-like, over-retouched, airbrushed skin,
symmetrical face, uncanny valley, stock photo pose, flat lighting, flash photography,
oversaturated colors, HDR effect, smooth skin without pores
```

---

## [B] Cinematográfico Narrativo (Cinematic Storytelling)

**Quando usar:** thumbnails, key visuals de vídeo, campanhas com storytelling, heroshots.

**Prompt Injection:**
```
shot on ARRI ALEXA 65 with Panavision Ultra Vista 50mm anamorphic lens,
teal and orange color grading, golden hour backlight with volumetric haze,
dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, god rays filtering through atmosphere,
film grain texture ISO 800, anamorphic lens flare, atmospheric depth with
bokeh particles, cinematic 2.39:1 framing, natural skin imperfections,
subsurface scattering, catch light in eyes reflecting environment,
emotional micro-expression, wind-touched hair with flyaway strands
```

**Negative Injection:**
```
watermark, deformed, extra fingers, plastic skin, illustration, cartoon,
flat lighting, studio backdrop, flash photography, over-processed, HDR,
smooth poreless skin, symmetrical face, stock photo, stiff pose, dead eyes,
oversaturated, airbrushed, uncanny valley
```

---

## [C] Corporativo Autêntico (Corporate & Institutional)

**Quando usar:** saúde, tecnologia, educação, financeiro, institucional. Credibilidade e conexão humana.

**Prompt Injection:**
```
shot on Sony A1 with 85mm f/1.4 GM lens, natural window light from camera left,
soft diffused fill from reflector, clean neutral background with subtle gradient,
genuine warm smile reaching the eyes (Duchenne smile), natural skin texture
with visible pores, slight asymmetry in features, professional attire with
subtle wrinkles in fabric, relaxed confident posture, eye-level camera angle,
medium close-up framing from chest up, neutral color temperature 5600K,
gentle catchlight in both eyes, natural makeup or no makeup look
```

**Negative Injection:**
```
watermark, deformed, extra fingers, plastic skin, illustration, cartoon,
glamour lighting, dramatic shadows, fashion pose, heavy makeup, airbrushed,
HDR, oversaturated, fake smile, stiff corporate pose, stock photo aesthetic,
overly perfect skin, uncanny valley, dead eyes
```

## [D] Emocional / Impacto Social (Emotional & Cause-Driven)

**Quando usar:** ONGs, causas sociais, campanhas de conscientização, saúde, storytelling emocional.

**Prompt Injection:**
```
shot on Leica SL2-S with 90mm Summicron f/2.0, available natural light,
documentary photography style, intimate close-up framing emphasizing eyes,
raw and unretouched skin texture with every line and mark visible,
genuine unposed expression, environmental context visible in soft bokeh background,
slightly desaturated color palette with warm shadows, subtle lens vignette,
film-like tonal curve with rich midtones, catch light reflecting real environment,
authentic body language, lived-in clothing with natural wear
```

**Negative Injection:**
```
watermark, deformed, extra fingers, plastic skin, illustration, cartoon,
studio lighting, glamour, fashion, airbrushed, retouched, HDR, oversaturated,
posed, fake emotion, stock photo, commercial aesthetic, perfect skin,
uncanny valley, heavy makeup
```
